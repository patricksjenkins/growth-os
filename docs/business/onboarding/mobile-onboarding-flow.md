# Mobile-Native Onboarding Flow (v1, 2026-05-15)

The path for a client to complete onboarding **entirely on their phone**
— either through a mobile-optimized web form OR inside their branded
FGA app. Most small service business owners live on their phone; this
flow respects that.

This document maps the **screen-by-screen UX** and the **data capture
sequencing** across the 7-day onboarding window.

For the broader 7-day timeline, see `client-onboarding-runbook.md`.

---

## Three Onboarding Surfaces

| Surface | Used For | When |
|---|---|---|
| **A. Mobile web** (`/onboarding` on marketing site) | Pre-tenant essentials (5-min form). Works on any phone browser. No download needed. | Day 0, immediately after contract signed |
| **B. Shared FGA Concierge app** (single app, FGA-branded) | Customers who haven't gotten their branded app yet. Bridges Day 0 → Day 2. Optional — most use surface A then C. | Day 0-2 fallback |
| **C. Customer's branded TestFlight app** | The bulk of intake (logo, photos, voice, brand voice, customer list, etc.). Real branded experience. | Day 2-5 |

The shared FGA Concierge app (surface B) exists as a fallback for
customers who don't want to fill out web forms but can't yet receive
TestFlight access (e.g. Apple ID setup pending). It's a single app in
the App Store for the entire onboarding population. Recommend
**deferring B to post-launch** — A + C cover 90%+ of cases.

---

## Data Capture Sequencing (What Gets Asked Where)

| Surface | Fields Captured |
|---|---|
| **A. Mobile web (5 min, Day 0)** | Business name, owner name, email, mobile, service area, vertical, GBP status (Y/N), FB+IG status (Y/N), legal entity name, D-U-N-S availability |
| **C. Branded app wizard (Days 2-5)** | Logo upload, brand colors, photo seed (20+), brand voice samples (3 sentences), key services, business hours, color preferences, customer list, partner list, GBP URL, social handles, voice rules customization |

The web form on Day 0 ONLY captures what's needed to (a) start tenant
provisioning, (b) trigger Apple Developer enrollment, and (c) generate
TestFlight assets. Everything else is in-app for a better experience.

## Surface A — Mobile Web Intake (Day 0)

### URL Structure

`https://firstgenautomate.com/onboarding?client_id=<uuid>`

The `client_id` is generated by Stripe webhook on setup-fee payment
and embedded in the welcome email link. The form is locked to that
client_id — opening the link in another browser tab still works
(it's just a UUID).

### What Happens On Submit

1. Form POSTs to `/api/onboarding/intake` (existing endpoint, extended
   to capture new fields)
2. Backend creates/updates tenant row, sets `status = 'onboarding'`
3. Backend queues `agent_jobs` for: Apple Developer enrollment email
   trigger, asset generation pipeline, vertical preset application
4. Browser shows success screen with:
   - "Thanks, [Owner Name]. Here's what happens next."
   - Inline 7-day timeline visual
   - Calendar link to book Day 5 founder call
   - "We'll text you when your branded app is ready to install on
     Day 1-2" (sets expectation for TestFlight invite)

## Surface C — In-App Onboarding Wizard (Days 2-5)

### Entry Point

Customer installs their branded TestFlight build (Day 1-2). On first
launch:

1. App loads `App.js` → checks `tenant_id` from build config
2. Calls backend `/api/tenant/onboarding-state` → returns
   `{ stage: 'in_app_intake_pending', completed_steps: [...] }`
3. App routes to `OnboardingWizardScreen` instead of normal home

The wizard is **resumable** — close the app, come back tomorrow, pick
up where you left off. Each step auto-saves to `tenant_config` on
completion.

### Skip + Resume Logic

| Step | Skippable? | If skipped... |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Welcome | No | — |
| 2 Logo | Yes | Use generic vertical icon, prompt again next launch |
| 3 Colors | Yes | Use FGA Midnight + Signal Green defaults |
| 4 Photo seed | Yes (but warn — content gen will be slow) | Generate stock-photo content from Gemini until seeded |
| 5 Voice | Yes (warn — voice will be generic) | Use vertical-preset voice |
| 6 Services + hours | No (needed for templates) | Block until provided |
| 7 GBP | Yes | Review module stays dormant until URL provided |
| 8 FB+IG | Yes | Content drafts queue but don't publish until connected |
| 9 Customer list | Yes | Referral + review modules dormant until populated |
| 10 Complete | — | — |

App treats "incomplete onboarding" as a non-blocking state. Customer
can use the app normally, but a soft banner reads "Finish setup to
unlock everything" on home screen until all required steps are done.

## State Machine

```
                                                  ┌──> branded_app_ready
                                                  │   (TestFlight invite sent)
                                                  │
[contract_signed] ──> [tenant_provisioned] ───────┼──> [in_app_intake_started]
                       (web form submitted)       │              │
                                                  │              ▼
                                                  │       [in_app_intake_complete]
                                                  │              │
                                                  └──> [founder_call_scheduled]
                                                          │
                                                          ▼
                                                  [founder_call_complete]
                                                          │
                                                          ▼
                                                       [active]
```

Stored in `tenants.status` + `tenant_config.onboarding_stage` for
fine-grained step tracking.

## Backend Endpoints Required

| Endpoint | Method | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `/api/onboarding/intake` | POST | Existing — web form Day 0 submit |
| `/api/tenant/onboarding-state` | GET | App reads on launch: what stage, what steps complete |
| `/api/tenant/onboarding-step` | POST | App writes when customer completes a wizard step |
| `/api/tenant/onboarding-complete` | POST | App signals "wizard done" → backend flips stage |
| `/api/tenant/upload-asset` | POST (multipart) | Logo, photo seed, etc. — pushes to Supabase Storage |
| `/api/tenant/connect-buffer` | POST | Buffer OAuth callback (mobile deep link) |
| `/api/tenant/connect-google` | POST | GBP claim flow (mobile deep link) |

**Net new endpoints to build:** 6 of 7 (intake already exists).
Estimate: ~3 hours engineering after the onboarding-state schema is
designed.

## Why This Mobile-Native Path Matters

Three reasons it's worth the engineering investment:

1. **Target customer behavior.** A 1-10 person service-business owner
   on a Tuesday afternoon has 12 minutes between jobs. They have their
   phone. They don't have their laptop. Onboarding has to happen
   wherever they are.

2. **Photo capture is the killer feature.** "Take a photo of your truck
   for your logo" or "select 20 photos from your camera roll for
   content" is a native-app moment, not a web-form moment. The web
   form on Day 0 stays minimal **because** the rest is better on
   mobile.

3. **It primes them for the daily-driver use of the app.** By the time
   they finish wizard step 10, they've already used the photo
   uploader, approved content, connected social — exactly the
   workflow they'll do every day. Onboarding IS the first day's
   product usage.

## Phasing — What to Ship When

Given the launch window:

| Phase | Ships | Components |
|---|---|---|
| **Phase 1 (pre-launch, ~3 hours)** | Mobile-web Day 0 form, all 6 backend endpoints, schema, basic state machine | Form + API + tenant_config patterns |
| **Phase 2 (week 1 post-launch, ~10 hours)** | Full in-app 10-step wizard | OnboardingWizardScreen + 10 step components |
| **Phase 3 (later)** | Shared FGA Concierge app (surface B) | Only if Phase 1+2 leave gaps |

**Recommendation:** Ship Phase 1 before the first customer signs (the
web form already works — just needs the 5 added fields). Phase 2 ships
during the first customer's onboarding window — customer uses email
+ web for the first run while the in-app wizard is built behind them.
