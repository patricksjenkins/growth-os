# Acceptable Use Policy — First Gen Automate

**Effective Date: May 22, 2026**

This Acceptable Use Policy ("**AUP**") governs Client's use of the First Gen Automate platform ("the **System**") operated by First Gen Automate LLC ("**Provider**"). The AUP is incorporated by reference into the Master Services Agreement.

By using the System, Client agrees to comply with this AUP. Violations may result in suspension or termination per the Master Services Agreement.

---

## 1. Prohibited Content

Client shall not use the System to create, store, send, or publish content that:

- Is unlawful, fraudulent, deceptive, or misleading
- Violates intellectual property rights (copyrights, trademarks, patents, trade secrets, publicity rights)
- Defames, libels, harasses, or invades the privacy of any person
- Contains hate speech, threats of violence, or incites unlawful action
- Promotes illegal drugs, illegal gambling, illegal firearms, or other unlawful goods or services
- Contains sexually explicit material, including content sexualizing minors
- Constitutes spam, chain letters, pyramid schemes, or unsolicited commercial communications outside the System's intended use
- Contains malware, viruses, worms, trojan horses, or other harmful code
- Is intended to phish for or steal credentials, personal information, or financial information

---

## 2. Prohibited Conduct

Client shall not:

- Use the System to send communications to recipients who have not consented (or for whom consent is not legally required) — TCPA, CAN-SPAM, and carrier-level consent rules apply
- Attempt to reverse-engineer, decompile, or otherwise extract the source code of the System
- Attempt to bypass or disable security features, rate limits, or access controls
- Use the System to compete directly with Provider (i.e., reselling the System as a Client's own platform)
- Misrepresent Client's identity or affiliation in communications the System sends
- Use the System for any "high-risk" or restricted use case prohibited by Telnyx, Buffer, Stripe, or Provider's other integration providers (see Section 4)
- Resell, sublicense, or transfer Client's account credentials
- Use automation, bots, or scripts to abuse the System beyond Client's authorized usage

---

## 3. SMS and Voice Communications

Because SMS and voice communications are heavily regulated and carrier-monitored:

### 3.1 Consent

Client warrants that every recipient of an SMS or voice communication sent through the System has either:

- (a) provided express consent to receive that type of communication from Client, or
- (b) is within an exception to consent requirements under applicable law (e.g., a strict customer-service response to an inbound inquiry).

### 3.2 Opt-Out

All recipient-facing SMS communications sent through the System must include a clear opt-out mechanism (typically "Reply STOP to opt out"). Client agrees not to configure or attempt to circumvent the System's automatic STOP/HELP handling.

### 3.3 Content Restrictions

The following content types are prohibited in SMS sent through the System without explicit prior written approval from Provider (carrier and aggregator restrictions):

- "SHAFT" content: Sex, Hate, Alcohol, Firearms, Tobacco/cannabis
- Loan offers, payday lending, debt collection
- Gambling and lottery promotions
- Multi-level marketing recruitment
- Cryptocurrency promotions
- Phishing or impersonation of other businesses

### 3.4 Volume

Client agrees to operate within the tier-specific volume limits documented at https://firstgenautomate.com/volume-limits. Repeated attempts to bypass these limits may result in account suspension.

## 5. Data Privacy

### 5.1 Customer Data

Client warrants that all customer/lead data submitted to the System (names, phone numbers, email addresses, addresses, etc.) was lawfully obtained.

### 5.2 No Sensitive Categories

Client shall not store or send the following categories of sensitive data through the System:

- Government-issued identification numbers (SSN, ITIN, driver's license, passport — except as may be necessary for Stripe-handled tax forms)
- Financial account numbers other than what Stripe manages for billing
- Protected health information (PHI) subject to HIPAA — unless Client has signed a Business Associate Agreement with Provider (BAA not currently offered)
- Information about individuals under 16 years of age

### 5.3 Data Subject Requests

If Client receives a request from a customer/lead to access, correct, or delete their data, Client is the data controller and is responsible for responding. Provider will assist Client in fulfilling such requests upon request.

## 6. Suspension and Termination

Provider reserves the right to suspend or terminate Client's account for violations of this AUP, including:

- Immediate suspension for Section 1 violations (prohibited content) or material legal/regulatory risk
- Notice + cure for first-occurrence violations of Sections 2-5
- Termination for repeated or willful violations

Provider will use reasonable efforts to notify Client of a violation before suspending service, unless immediate action is required to comply with law or protect the platform.
