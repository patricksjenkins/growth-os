## 1. Services

### 1.1 The System

Provider operates a done-for-you business operating system ("the **System**") that automates lead capture, lead follow-up, review requests, referral generation, content creation, content approval, and social media publishing for small businesses. The System is delivered as a branded mobile application and web portal, configured and operated by Provider on behalf of Client.

### 1.2 Scope

The specific modules, volume limits, and deliverables included in Client's subscription are set forth in the Scope of Work executed by the Parties ("**SOW**"), which is incorporated by reference into this Agreement. Modules and limits are described in Provider's then-current Module Catalog at https://firstgenautomate.com.

### 1.3 Service Tiers

| Tier | Setup Fee | Monthly Fee | Modules |
|---|---|---|---|
| Growth | $199 one-time | $249/month | Any 7 of 15 modules |
| Scale | $199 one-time | $399/month | All 15 modules |

Volume limits and overage policy are documented at https://firstgenautomate.com/volume-limits and incorporated by reference.

### 1.4 Free Trial

Client subscribes to a **14-day free trial** of the recurring monthly subscription. Client provides payment information at signup. The $199 setup fee is charged immediately upon execution of this Agreement. The recurring monthly subscription fee is not billed until day 15 of the trial. Client may cancel during the trial at no recurring cost. The setup fee is non-refundable once Provider has begun work, which typically occurs on Day 1.

## 2. Payment

### 2.1 Fees

Client agrees to pay the fees set forth in the SOW. All fees are billed in advance via the payment method on file (typically credit/debit card via Stripe).

### 2.2 Auto-Renewal

The monthly subscription auto-renews each calendar month unless Client cancels in accordance with Section 8.

### 2.3 Late Payment

If a payment fails, Provider's billing system will attempt re-processing per Stripe's standard dunning sequence (4 attempts over ~14 days). If all attempts fail, the System may be suspended until payment is cured. Service suspension does not waive accrued fees.

### 2.4 Taxes

Fees do not include applicable taxes. Client is responsible for any applicable sales, use, or similar taxes other than taxes based on Provider's net income.

## 6. Term and Termination

### 6.1 Term

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues until terminated.

### 6.2 Termination by Client

Client may terminate at any time via the cancellation flow in the System or by written notice to Provider. Termination takes effect at the end of the current billing period.

### 6.3 Termination by Provider

Provider may terminate or suspend service for: (a) non-payment after the dunning sequence completes; (b) material breach of this Agreement or the Acceptable Use Policy; (c) use of the System for unlawful purposes; (d) abuse of the integration providers' (Twilio, Buffer, etc.) terms of service.

### 6.4 Effect of Termination

Upon termination:
- Provider will provide Client a 90-day window to export Client data via the System
- After 90 days, all Client data is permanently deleted from Provider's systems and backups
- All outstanding fees become due immediately
- The branded mobile application's authentication transitions to a cancellation-screen state; the listing is later removed from the App Store

### 6.5 Survival

Sections 2 (Payment), 4 (Intellectual Property), 5 (Confidentiality), 7 (Disclaimers), 8 (Indemnification), 9 (Limitation of Liability), 10 (Dispute Resolution), and 12 (General) survive termination.
